The Study Abroad
Experience





Our Study Abroad program lasted from September 1st to December 12th, taking up the entire Fall semester from WMU! Our program had a variety of exciting classes and experiences for us to familiarize ourselves with the culture of Spain.

  • Our entire learning experience was in the Spanish language. We had to speak it and read it in every lesson, lecture, and guided tour. It was a thorough test of our comprehension and everything we had learned in our Spanish classes up to that point. We had one class that lasted the entire semester that was focused on strengthening our language comprehension through grammar and vocabulary lessons.

  • We had a few classes that were only a couple weeks long. History, Geography, Art, and Music gave us knowledge of all the eras and movements that spain went through in these categories over the years and the impacts they had on the culture and identity of Spain. We had a couple experiences related to each of these classes as well, such as seeing some fossil records at the Atapuerca archaeological dig and visiting some art and historical museums in old cities in the country.

  • We had a couple experience-focused classes that were only a couple days long. We had a cooking class that exposed us to many foods that originated in Spain, like Paella, Sangria, and Morcilla de Burgos. We also had a dance class where we learned to Flamenco, and we learned the first movement of La Sevillana. Combining the steps with the complex hand movements was a serious undertaking that only the best of us got good at!

As for accommodations, I stayed with a host family. Rather, I stayed with a single host mom. Her kids were grown up and had moved out of her apartment, so it was just me and her! She helped me practice my Spanish outside of class, and I helped her practice her English since she was just starting to learn it. We lived right next to a busy roundabout in the city, so I had to get used to the sounds of traffic lulling me to sleep each night.

Finally, since we were in Spain through early December, we decided to have a joint Thanksgiving/Christmas pajama party! We each brought a dish that we cooked at our host homes, and put on Home Alone in the background while decorating cookies. This is my personal favorite memory from the whole experience because it was just such a fun party and a special night we all shared together!
