My Trip to
Barcelona





Barcelona was a different experience for me because I visited here twice, separate from the Study Abroad experience. I went here with my family on vacation prior to the start of the program, then I went back when we got a week vacation in the fall. Barcelona is a coastal city, and it has a noticeably different culture from Burgos and Madrid.

Barcelona emulates a tropical environment. In the summer, the weather is hot, so people flock to the beach to enjoy the salty sea, shade from the palm trees, and ice cold drinks from the various cabanas and restaurants lining the shore.

It was cooler in the fall, which was more ideal for walking around the city and exploring its rich history. It used to be part of Catalonia, a separately recognized region during the Roman era. Many people in Barcelona still feel distinct from the rest of Spain, and calls for official independence stir up again every couple of years.

Barcelona strongly exudes its identity through art history. The movements from famous musicians, painters, architects, and once again the Romans have all been captured and immortalized in various historical museums and buildings around the city.

The centerpiece of the city is certainly La Sagrada Familia, a gigantic cathedral designed by Gaudí that hosts thousands of people everyday for church.




Montserrat



Barcelona is not only a city, but also a region. Within it lies an incredible mountain range called Montserrat. This mountain range is not only known for its natural beauty and incredible views, but it is also an important religious site. It hosts the Benedictine abbey Santa Maria de Montserrat, which has a sanctuary and village that is visited by hundreds of thousands of people every year.


A cable car takes us up to the village, which is the starting point of all hiking paths.

Near the beginning of the hiking paths, there are some religious shrines and sculptures...

...but the further you go, the path becomes just nature. I wasn't kidding about the incredible views!


Montserrat is an absolute must-see if you enjoy nature, exercise, and a day of good weather!
